About

The 26 Club

The Montana Institute of Sport is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to bettering young Montanans through physical education — curriculum support, teaching grants, and professional development for the state's public schools.

The 26 Club is our circle of founding partners. This members' hub is where we share exactly how your support is translating into impact on the ground, month after month.
