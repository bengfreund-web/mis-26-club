What We're About

Better young Montanans through physical education

The Montana Institute of Sport is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to improving physical education in Montana's public schools — through curriculum support, teaching grants, and professional development. It was founded in 2022 by Ali Behnam and JD Stephenson.

The 26 Club takes its name from a hard truth: only 26% of young people get the recommended daily amount of physical activity. It's an exclusive, invite-only community of up to 26 members who believe that number can change — starting in Montana.

What It Supports
Where your membership goes

Membership powers the TRY Sport initiative — introducing Montana kids to new and popular sports, and giving their teachers the curriculum and tools to keep them moving.

01
Montana's public schools need help
Facing tight budgets, schools are struggling to fund physical education and engage students in healthy, active habits.
02
Teachers deserve better support
Montana sits below the national average for teacher compensation and public-school support. We provide curriculum, equipment, clinics, and professional development.
03
Kids need to move
With only 26% of students meeting daily activity guidelines, TRY Sport brings new sports and lasting habits into classrooms across the state.
