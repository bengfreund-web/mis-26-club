The Mission

Better young Montanans through physical education

The Montana Institute of Sport is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit improving physical education in Montana's public schools — through curriculum support, teaching grants, and professional development. The 26 Club is its inner circle: a small group of members who put their energy and generosity behind that work.

The name comes from a simple statistic — only 26% of young people get the daily activity they need. Every membership helps change it, and brings the members along for the ride.

Where It Goes
Every membership powers TRY Sport

TRY Sport introduces Montana kids to new sports and gives their teachers the tools to keep them moving.

Tight budgets have left physical education underfunded, and too many kids without the chance to fall in love with a sport.

Teachers need backing
We provide curriculum, equipment, clinics, and professional development so the people in the gym have what they need.
03 /
Kids need to move
With only 26% of students meeting daily activity guidelines, TRY Sport builds new sports and lasting habits into classrooms statewide.
