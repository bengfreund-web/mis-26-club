The Club

An exclusive circle, by design

The 26 Club is an invitation-only community of like-minded men who believe in the power of sport. Its name comes from a hard truth: only 26% of young people get the daily activity they need. Membership is deliberately capped at twenty-six.

"Twenty-six seats. When they're full, they're full."

Founded within the Montana Institute of Sport in 2022, the club channels the energy, generosity, and reach of its members into real change for kids across the state — while giving those members a brotherhood, and a calendar, unlike any other.

The Purpose
Where your seat goes

Membership powers the TRY Sport initiative — introducing Montana kids to new sports and giving their teachers the tools to keep them moving.

01
Montana's schools need help
Tight budgets have left physical education underfunded, and too many kids without the chance to fall in love with a sport.
02
Teachers deserve backing
We provide curriculum, equipment, clinics, and professional development so the people in the gym have what they need to succeed.
03
Kids need to move
With only 26% of students meeting daily activity guidelines, TRY Sport builds new sports and lasting habits into classrooms across the state.
