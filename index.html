Our Impact
What we've built together

The 26 Club is the inner circle of the Montana Institute of Sport — members whose support puts kids in motion across the state.

Where Your Dollars Go
Every membership powers TRY Sport

TRY Sport introduces Montana kids to new sports and gives their teachers the tools to keep them moving.

01 /
Into the schools
Curriculum, equipment, and on-site clinics land directly in Montana's public schools — where physical education has been underfunded for years.
02 /
Behind the teachers
Teaching grants and professional development back the people in the gym, so the impact outlasts any single season.
03 /
Onto the kids
Only 26% of young people get the daily activity they need. Every membership helps move that number — one classroom at a time.
